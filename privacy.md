# Privacy Policy – Understanding Superhero Comic Books: The Publishing Game

This game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No account is required.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
- The game never connects to the internet.
- Saved progress and best scores are stored only on the device (local storage inside the app). Deleting the app deletes them.
- The game does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other device sensors.
